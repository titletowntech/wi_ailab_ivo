# Welcome to App Xchange

> Source: https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/welcome-to-app-xchange
> Captured directly from rendered HTML: 2026-08-14

App Xchange
        is a platform that facilitates the flow of data between different apps within the
        construction industry and beyond. It helps solve common problems that occur when the same or
        similar data exists between many apps.

Have you ever had to add the same project information in both Vista and ProjectSight? App Xchange's flows automate processes like these to ensure you only have to enter the data
            once.

![App Xchange flow builder](https://help.trimble.com/dist/suf/product-guides/heretto/app-xchange/en/app-xchange/7aec1777-ec66-43bf-a73b-d0a6ef1253c3.png)

App Xchange creates
            connections between services to pull data from one service to the other through flows.
            Flows can filter, combine, extract, or group your data. The data can then be leveraged
            by an integration that sends the data to different apps or emails it to vested partners.
            Integration builders can even sell their integrations to customers on Trimble Marketplace.

## Who is App Xchange For?

App Xchange is for
                anyone in the construction tech industry who needs to integrate their data with
                other systems. We cater to a wide range of businesses in the construction industry
                and have key partnerships with software vendors that makes it easier to integrate
                your data.

Focusing on the construction tech industry has provided great
                insight into how our customers and partners operate, and it shows in our platform
                and products.

## What Can I Do with App Xchange?

App Xchange is built
                to offer flexibility and ease of use. Its tools ensure a smooth and fast transition
                from project conception to planning, deployment, and maintenance. Here are some of
                the things you can do using App Xchange.

- Keep your integrations and data separated with dedicated
                          workspaces.
- Customize out-of-the-box flows or use them ad-hoc to
                          transfer data.
- Build your own flows from start to finish in a low-code
                          environment.
- Reuse flows across multiple workspaces with custom
                          configurations.
- Use a growing list of connectors to integrate new
                          software.

To learn more about tne first steps requires to accomplish these
                tasks, see [Getting Started](https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/getting-started).
