# Account Settings

> Source: https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/administration/your-app-xchange-account/account-settings
> Captured directly from rendered HTML: 2026-08-14

The Settings page displays key information about your company account and which of its account features are currently enabled.

## Account Info

This section displays your account name, the user that created it, and the date it was created.

## Account Features

This section details which supplemental account features are active for your company account. The toggle button icon indicates the status of the feature. Individual users must also have these permissions to use them. An individual user may have permissions for a feature, but if the account does not have those same permissions, then the user will not have access. If you previously had access to a feature, you may still have that access even if your account permissions have changed. Contact your sales representative to make changes to your account features.

These are the currently offered account features and the actions they enable.
