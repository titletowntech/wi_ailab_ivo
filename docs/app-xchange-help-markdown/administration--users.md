# Users

> Source: https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/administration/users
> Captured directly from rendered HTML: 2026-08-14

The ![users icon](https://help.trimble.com/dist/suf/product-guides/heretto/app-xchange/en/app-xchange/48fc7c78-195d-498a-8318-185fda489821.svg) Users page is where you can view all App Xchange users within your company account and its workspaces.

You can view and manage users from the ![users icon](https://help.trimble.com/dist/suf/product-guides/heretto/app-xchange/en/app-xchange/48fc7c78-195d-498a-8318-185fda489821.svg) Users page, which is found on the left navigation pane within
   your company account. Here you can view user information across several tabs.

## Users Tab

This tab lists the names, emails, and permissions of all users on your
    company account. Administrators can select a user to edit their email, name, phone number, and
     [permissions](https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/administration/users/permissions). Administrators can also lock the accounts
    of inactive users to limit their access to the portal and secure API endpoints.

## Service Accounts Tab

This tab lists the names, emails, and permissions of all Service Account users on your company account. Service Account users are almost exclusively used for connectors that require restricted access to the App Xchange platform.

## Invites Tab

This tab lists the email addresses of invited users and the date their invitations expire. Select the Show All? checkbox to see expired invitations. Administrators can select a specific invite to modify, resend, or revoke it.
