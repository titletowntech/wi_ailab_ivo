# List your Integration on Trimble Marketplace

> Source: https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/integrations/list-your-integration-on-trimble-marketplace
> Captured directly from rendered HTML: 2026-08-14

Certified integration products built on the App Xchange platform can be listed and
        co-sold on Trimble Marketplace, allowing you to earn revenue with your product.

                To apply for a Trimble Marketplace listing, [sign up directly on Trimble
                        Marketplace](https://marketplace.trimble.com/en-US/pages/marketplace-partnership).

The benefits of listing your integration include a
                        dedicated product page for your connected solution, invitations to Trimble
                        user and partner conferences, and more.

                        Note: Integrations built on
                                App Xchange will have
                            their annual listing fees waived. Revenue sharing still applies.
