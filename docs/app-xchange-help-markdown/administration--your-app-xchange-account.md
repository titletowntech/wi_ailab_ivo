# Your App Xchange Account

> Source: https://help.trimble.com/doc/app-xchange/app-xchange/administration/your-app-xchange-account
> Captured directly from rendered HTML: 2026-08-14

Your App Xchange account represents your company and all the resources you own on the App Xchange platform.

## Quick-Access Icons

The App Xchange user
                interface contains several quick-access icons for account-level utilities.

- Select the magnifying glass icon
                          to toggle dark mode.
- Select the sun/moon icon to
                          toggle dark mode.
- Select the bell icon to check
                          your notifications.
- Select your user icon to review
                          your account details.
- Select Switch to view any additional business accounts you are
                          registered to.
- Select Log Out to log out.
